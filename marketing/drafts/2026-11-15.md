## Before posting

- "Net tax owing" is CRA's term (roughly: tax for the year minus tax already withheld and certain credits) — it's not total income tax. Keep the exact words; don't shorten to "owe over $3,000 in tax".
- The hook says "Owe CRA over $3,000?" as a question; the body adds the second condition (a prior year too). Don't drop that line — it's what makes the claim accurate.
- The Quebec $1,800 threshold depends on where you live on December 31 (CRA's wording). The video says "Quebec: over $1,800" for length; the pin description has the full wording.
- Interest *rates* and how instalment interest is calculated were left out (not checked today). Farmers/fishers (single Dec 31 date) were left out.
- Dec 15, 2026 is a Tuesday — no weekend shift needed.
- Comment prompt is a one-tap "Reply yes or no", per the 11-14 review note on zero comments.

## Sources checked (2026-11-15)

- CRA, "Who has to pay — Required tax instalments for individuals" (date modified 2026-01-20) — instalments may be needed if net tax owing is more than $3,000 ($1,800 in Quebec, based on where you live on Dec 31) for 2026 and in either 2025 or 2024; reminders (INNS1) sent in February (March/June) and August (September/December); at or below the threshold → no instalments needed even with a reminder: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/payments/payments-cra/individual-payments/income-tax-instalments/who-pays-instalments.html
- CRA, "Payment due dates — Required tax instalments for individuals" (date modified 2026-01-20) — March 15, June 15, September 15, December 15; weekend/holiday → next business day counts as on time; late or insufficient payments can result in instalment interest and penalties; farmers/fishers: December 31: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/payments/payments-cra/individual-payments/income-tax-instalments/due-dates.html
- CRA, "Required tax instalments for individuals" (date modified 2026-01-20) — instalments are payments made during the year toward tax otherwise paid in one lump sum by April 30 of the following year: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/payments/payments-cra/individual-payments/income-tax-instalments.html
- Canada.ca, "2027 Tax deadlines for Canadian businesses and self-employed individuals" (date modified 2026-10-16) — self-employed: file by June 15, 2027, balance due April 30, 2027; instalments March 15, June 15, September 15, December 15: https://www.canada.ca/en/services/taxes/resources-for-small-and-medium-businesses/2026-tax-deadlines-canadian-businesses-self-employed-individuals.html
