## Before posting

- No rates, limits or deadlines today on purpose. The one-line "jobs" are CRA/canada.ca's own descriptions (sources below); don't add tax treatment (deductions, tax-free withdrawals) without checking it the same day.
- "TFSA → any goal, any time" is CRA's "withdraw at any time, for any reason". It doesn't mean withdrawals give you room back right away — keep it to the purpose only.
- 401(k), Roth IRA and 529 plan are named only as US account names, not as "equivalents" of Canadian accounts — don't add "RRSP = 401(k)"-style comparisons.
- Product claims come from `site/src/products.json` (monthly plan in CAD; TFSA, RRSP, FHSA, RESP and emergency fund savings lines; twelve months side by side; Google Sheets copy + Excel). Don't say it does more than that.

## Sources checked (2026-11-19)

- CRA, "What is a TFSA" (date modified 2026-09-17) — you can withdraw funds "at any time, for any reason": https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/tax-free-savings-account/what.html
- CRA, "Registered Retirement Savings Plan (RRSP)" (date modified 2026-05-07) — "a retirement savings plan that you establish, that the CRA registers": https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsps-related-plans/registered-retirement-savings-plan-rrsp.html
- CRA, "Make the most of your first home savings account" (tax tip, date modified 2025-07-22) — "FHSAs are intended for people who are saving for their first home": https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/news/newsroom/tax-tips/tax-tips-2025/make-most-your-first-home-savings-account.html
- Canada.ca, "Registered Education Savings Plans and related benefits" (date modified 2026-11-18) — "a long-term savings plan to help people save for a child's education after high school": https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/education/education-savings.html
