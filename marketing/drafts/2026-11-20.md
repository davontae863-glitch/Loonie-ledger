## Before posting

- "Online ad → 8521": CRA's Canadian-content/ownership requirements for advertising "do not apply if you advertise on foreign websites"; ads aimed mainly at Canada with a foreign *broadcaster* aren't deductible. Keep it to "online ad" — don't generalize to TV/radio/print.
- "Usually only half counts" / "generally capped at 50%": CRA's rule is 50% of the lesser of the amount spent or a reasonable amount, with exceptions (e.g. meals billed to a client, businesses that sell food/entertainment, long-haul truckers at 80%). Don't say "always 50%".
- 9220: CRA says you can deduct "the percentage of airtime expenses for a cellphone you use to earn your business income"; the basic monthly rate of a home phone isn't deductible (a separate business-only line is). The video says "business share" on purpose.
- CCA: CRA's 9936 page says you can't deduct the cost of depreciable property in the year, but can deduct it over several years. The CCA *class and rate* for furniture wasn't checked today — don't add one. Line 9936 is the CCA line on the T2125.
- Left out (not checked today): software/subscription lines (CRA's pages read today don't name a line for software), 8811 office stationery and supplies vs 8810 (the guide separates them — 8811 is for items used to provide your goods/services), the half-year rule, and any dollar amounts.
