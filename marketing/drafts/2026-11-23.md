## Before posting

- The year-first format is **our recommendation**, not a CRA requirement. None of the CRA pages read today (RC188, "Where to keep your records…", "GST/HST records to keep", RC4022 partly) mention invoice numbers or a numbering format. Don't add "CRA requires invoice numbers". (Revenu Québec rules weren't checked — don't mention Quebec.)
- "Keep them 6 years": CRA's GST/HST page says "Usually, you must keep your records for 6 years from the end of the year to which they relate" and that CRA "may ask you to keep the invoices longer". The pin note drops "usually" for space; the caption and description keep it. Destroying records early needs written CRA permission — left out.
- "CRA lists sales invoices as GST/HST records": the "GST/HST records to keep" page lists "Sales and purchase invoices". This page is for GST/HST registrants; for non-registrants we only rely on the general 6-year rule (keeping-records page), so the wording stays "GST/HST records".
- Product claims come from `site/src/products.json`: "automatic numbering" on the invoice template, an invoice register with paid/unpaid status. Don't claim it customizes the number format or flags gaps — the video's "gaps jump out" is about the numbering habit, not a product feature.
- "Almost every new freelancer…" appears only in the internal "Why it stops the scroll" note, not in any caption or on screen.
