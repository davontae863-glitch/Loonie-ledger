## Before posting

- Script step 3 stays deliberately general ("a short window"). CRA's Small suppliers page says you stay a small supplier until the end of the month after the fourth quarter; its examples say 29 days to register while the quoted law says 30 — don't put a day count on screen.

## Sources checked (2026-11-02)

- CRA, GST/HST Memorandum 2-2 "Small suppliers" (date modified 2026-06-03) — $30,000 threshold over four calendar quarters; exceeding it in a single quarter ends small-supplier status immediately; exceeding it over four quarters keeps you a small supplier until the end of the following month: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/2-2/small-suppliers.html
- CRA, "When to register for and start charging the GST/HST" (cited 2026-10-31; could not be re-fetched today): https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html
