## Before posting

- "It's last year's" is the short form of CRA's wording: the CRA-account TFSA info is updated once a year (in spring) and reflects the previous year's transactions; financial institutions report by the end of February of the following year. It's not "wrong" — it just doesn't include this year. Keep scenes 2–4 so the hook is explained.
- "1% a month" (scene 5) is the TFSA rule: 1% per month on the highest excess for each month it stays in the account. FHSA is the same rate. RRSP is different — 1% a month only on unused contributions more than $2,000 over your deduction limit (CRA's page says the $2,000 applies if you were "18 or older at any time in 2024" — check the current year's wording). The pin note and pin description say this; the video only shows the TFSA rule on purpose.
- RRSP: CRA lists the notice of assessment, Form T1028, your CRA account and the automated phone line; the video names only the notice of assessment for length.
- FHSA: CRA says the participation room statement is "in most cases" on your latest notice of assessment (also reassessment or T1028), and in your CRA account (Savings and pension plans → View FHSA details).
- Left out (not checked today): the TFSA withdrawal re-contribution timing, the 2027 dollar limits, how to remove an excess, and Form RC243 / T1-OVP / RC728 filing details.
- The Room Tracker is described only as keeping all three rooms and this year's deposits in one sheet — don't say it pulls CRA data or calculates penalties unless the owner confirms it does.
