## Before posting

- This is the **self-employed** rule (Form T2125: vehicle expenses in Chart A, claimed on line 9281; CCA goes on line 9936). Employees use different rules — keep "self-employed" in the caption.
- The 27,000 / 30,000 km / $7,000 = $6,300 numbers are CRA's own worked example (the "Murray" example), not ours — keep the "CRA's example" label on screen.
- The calendar's "No log, no deduction" wasn't used: CRA's page doesn't state that consequence; it says expenses must be reasonable and supported, and use is determined from "an actual record of travel or alternative records".
- Left out for length: commuting from home to a regular workplace (CRA's page only addresses it for farming/fishing), passenger-vehicle CCA/interest/lease limits, and full-deductible business parking. Worth a future post.
- The Complete Bundle's vehicle log ("business kilometres by trip with a running business-use percentage") is from `site/src/products.json` — confirm the Payhip bundle listing matches before posting.

## Sources checked (2026-11-11)

- CRA, "Motor vehicle records" (date modified 2026-08-31) — best evidence is an accurate logbook of business travel for the entire year; for each business trip log the date, destination, purpose and kilometres; record the odometer at the start and end of the fiscal period; after a full one-year base-year logbook, a three-month sample logbook can be used if results are within 10% of the base year: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-expenses/motor-vehicle-expenses/motor-vehicle-records.html
- CRA, "Motor vehicle expenses" (date modified 2026-08-17) — deductible: licence and registration fees, fuel and oil, insurance, maintenance and repairs, leasing costs, among others; mixed use → only the business portion (business km ÷ total km); example 27,000 ÷ 30,000 × $7,000 = $6,300, claimed on T2125 line 9281 (Chart A); CCA on line 9936: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed-income/business-income-tax-reporting/business-expenses/motor-vehicle-expenses.html
