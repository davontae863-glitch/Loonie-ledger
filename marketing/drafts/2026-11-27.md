## Before posting

- Don't say "go paperless", "a photo is all you need" or "receipt apps are CRA-approved". CRA's page: "Generally, you must keep originals of your paper documents." Images become the permanent records only if imaged to the CGSB standard. Keep "generally" and "only if".
- The CGSB standard is named but not explained. IC05-1R1 lists what an acceptable imaging program needs (written confirmation, documented procedures, a logbook, an index, quality control); that's too much for a 15 s video and is left out — don't summarise it as easy or hard.
- "Blurry, faded or too dark" paraphrases CRA's "significant details of the image are not obscured because of limitations in resolution, tonality or hue." Don't upgrade to "a blurry photo gets your claim denied" — not checked.
- "Emailed receipt? Keep the digital file — even if you print it" follows CRA: records kept electronically must be kept "in an electronic readable format, even if you have paper printouts", and IC05-1R1: documents "received electronically… must also be retained electronically".
- "Back up your files" is CRA's "should" (recommendation), not a legal requirement — keep it as a tip, not a rule.
- Product claim from `site/src/products.json`: expense log with every row tagged with its T2125 line. The tracker has **no** receipt-photo/attachment feature — don't imply it stores receipts.
