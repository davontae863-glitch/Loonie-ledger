## Before posting

- Applies to **GST/HST registrants only** — the lead, caption and description all say "registered"/"if you're registered". Small suppliers who aren't registered don't charge it. Keep that qualifier.
- "Held in trust" and "can't use it as cash flow" are CRA's own words (deemed-trust page). Don't upgrade to penalties or collections claims — not checked today.
- "Separate spot" in the comment prompt is a question, not advice; CRA's page notes the amounts are "considered to be held separate and apart" even when mixed with your funds, so don't say CRA requires a separate bank account.
- 13% = Ontario HST per CRA's rate table (current column "On or after April 1, 2025"). All dollar amounts are a labelled **example** — keep "Example" on screen and "Example only" in the caption.
- ITC line: "deduct input tax credits from the GST/HST you collected" follows CRA's wording. Filing frequency (monthly/quarterly/annual) is left out — "each period" only.
- Product claims from `site/src/products.json`: income log with "GST/HST charged" column; province selector with GST, HST, PST and QST rates. Don't claim it files returns or calculates net tax owing.
