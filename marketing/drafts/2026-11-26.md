## Sources checked (2026-11-26)

- CRA, "When to register for and start charging the GST/HST" (date modified 2026-06-16) — "You exceed the $30,000 threshold in a single calendar quarter."; "You exceed the $30,000 threshold over the previous four (or fewer) consecutive calendar quarters (but not in a single calendar quarter)."; for small suppliers: "You do not have to register." / "You may choose to register voluntarily…"; footnote: "…from all your businesses and those of your associates…"; "Calendar quarter means a period of three months beginning on the first day of January, April, July, or October…": https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html
- CRA, GST/HST Memorandum 2-2 "Small suppliers" (June 2026, date modified 2026-06-03) — "A person that is a small supplier is not required to be registered for the GST/HST."; threshold "does not exceed $30,000 or, where the person is a public service body, $50,000": https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/2-2/small-suppliers.html
