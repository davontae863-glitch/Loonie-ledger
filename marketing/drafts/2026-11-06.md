## Before posting

- Keep "usually" on the services line. CRA's rule is that a service is supplied in the province of the client's Canadian address **when you get one in the normal course of business**; without an address the rule looks at where the service is performed. Goods follow where they're delivered — not covered in the video.
- No PST rates on screen on purpose: canada.ca sends PST questions to each province's website and QST to Revenu Québec, so we didn't verify those rates today. Quebec GST + QST is day 12.
- "All other provinces & territories: 5% GST" = Alberta, BC, Manitoba, Quebec, Saskatchewan, Yukon, NWT, Nunavut (per CRA's rate table today).
- Nova Scotia is 14% (since April 1, 2025), not 15%. Older CRA publications (e.g. 18-2, 2020) still say 15% — don't copy from those.
- Check the series number against posted.md before posting.
