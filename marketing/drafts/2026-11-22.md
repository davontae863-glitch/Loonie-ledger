## Before posting

- Don't name Etsy, Amazon, Uber or any other company as a reporting platform: CRA's pages list *activities*, not companies, and we couldn't confirm a specific platform on canada.ca today.
- "Under 30 sales + $2,800 or less": CRA's exact wording is "less than 30 relevant activities for the sale of goods and for which the total amount of consideration paid or credited did not exceed $2,800 during the reportable period". Both conditions must be true, and it applies to **sales of goods only** — not rentals, rides or services. The video says "sales of goods" on screen; keep that.
- "Business income still goes on your return": CRA's peer-to-peer page says to "Report and pay tax on all your income earned from peer-to-peer sales as self-employment income", and the digital-platforms page says sellers "may still have other tax obligations". We didn't say every sale of a used personal item is income — CRA's pages read today don't give a hobby/personal-items test, so don't add one.
- "You get a copy by January 31": CRA says operators must give each reportable seller a copy "by January 31 of the year following the calendar year" — only reportable sellers get one.
- "$500 penalty for each failure" (caption only): from CRA's "What information is shared" page, for not giving your TIN to the platform. It says "may", so keep "can charge".
- GST/HST end line: "more than $30,000 over four calendar quarters" — CRA peer-to-peer page (2025-01-31). Not re-explaining the threshold beyond one line.
- Left out (not checked or not needed): the first reporting year (2024, first deadline Jan 31, 2025 — true per CRA but adds nothing), the 2,000-rental exclusion for entity sellers, partner-jurisdiction sharing, and any claim about how CRA uses the data.
- Product claim from `site/src/products.json`: Income log (date, client, amount, GST/HST) and a small-supplier tracker with a running four-quarter total against $30,000. Don't say it imports platform reports.

## Sources checked (2026-11-22)

- CRA, "Reporting Rules for Digital Platforms" (date modified 2025-08-01) — platform operators "have to collect detailed information on certain sellers" and report it to CRA annually; relevant activities: sale of goods, rental of real or immovable property, personal services, rental of a means of transport: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/reporting-rules-digital-platforms.html
- CRA, "What information is shared" (date modified 2024-12-09) — reportable elements (name, primary address, TIN incl. SIN or business number, date of birth, consideration by calendar quarter, number of activities, fees by quarter); seller copy by January 31; "$500 for each failure to provide your TIN": https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/reporting-rules-digital-platforms/information-shared.html
- CRA, "Who is affected" (date modified 2024-11-12) — excluded seller: "less than 30 relevant activities for the sale of goods and for which the total amount of consideration paid or credited did not exceed $2,800": https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/reporting-rules-digital-platforms/who-is-affected.html
- CRA, "Filing information returns" (date modified 2026-05-05) — Part XX return due by January 31 for the previous calendar year; seller's role complete once information is shared, but "you may still have other tax obligations": https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/reporting-rules-digital-platforms/filing-information-returns.html
- CRA tax tip, "Using an app or website to earn income?" (2024-09-11) — platform operators give sellers a copy by January 31 each year: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/news/newsroom/tax-tips/tax-tips-2024/using-app-website-earn-income.html
- CRA, "Peer-to-peer" sellers (date modified 2025-01-31) — report all income from peer-to-peer sales as self-employment income on Form T2125; keep records; GST/HST registration generally over $30,000 over four calendar quarters: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/platform-economy/peer-to-peer.html
