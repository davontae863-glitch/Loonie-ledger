## Before posting

- Don't suggest scanning receipts and throwing out the paper. CRA's pages checked today don't cover imaging rules — that's day 27's topic and needs its own check. The script deliberately says "keep the originals".
- "Six years from the end of the tax year" is the general minimum; CRA notes exceptions (e.g. permission to destroy early, late-filed returns). Keep the wording "minimum" and don't add more.
- Records must be kept at your place of business or residence in Canada unless CRA approves otherwise — left off the post to keep it short; don't contradict it (e.g. no "store them anywhere in the cloud" lines).
- Use dummy data in the screen recording.
