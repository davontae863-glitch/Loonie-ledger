## Before posting

- "Tax-free" for the FHSA only applies to a **qualifying** withdrawal (CRA's conditions for buying a qualifying first home); other FHSA withdrawals are taxable. Scene 6 and the caption say this — don't cut them.
- "Deduction" for the FHSA and RRSP: CRA says contributions are "generally deductible" up to your limits. TFSA "tax-free" is also CRA's "generally". Kept short on screen; the pin description has the wording.
- RRSP $33,810 is the 2026 *dollar limit*, not everyone's room — your limit is generally 18% of last year's earned income up to that, plus unused room, adjusted for pension adjustments. Video says "up to $33,810" on purpose.
- FHSA $8,000 is the room in the year you open your first FHSA (later years: up to $8,000 of new room plus up to $8,000 carried forward, within the $40,000 lifetime cap). The pin note says "$8,000 a year, $40,000 total" for space — acceptable shorthand, but the description has the exact wording.

## Sources checked (2026-11-17)

- CRA, "What is a TFSA" (date modified 2026-09-17) — contributions not tax deductible; contributions and income generally tax-free, even when withdrawn; withdraw at any time, for any reason: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/tax-free-savings-account/what.html
- CRA, "MP, DB, RRSP, DPSP, ALDA, TFSA limits, YMPE and the YAMPE" (date modified 2026-10-28) — TFSA dollar limit 2026 $7,000; RRSP dollar limit 2026 $33,810: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/registered-plans-administrators/pspa/mp-rrsp-dpsp-tfsa-limits-ympe.html
- CRA, "How contributions affect your RRSP deduction limit" (date modified 2026-01-29) — deduction limit is generally unused room plus the lesser of 18% of previous year's earned income or the annual RRSP limit, adjusted for PA/PAR/PSPA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsps-related-plans/contributing-a-rrsp-prpp/contributions-affect-your-rrsp-prpp-deduction-limit.html
- CRA, Guide T4040 "RRSPs and Other Registered Plans for Retirement" (date modified 2026-01-23) — RRSP contributions deductible up to the limits; you generally pay tax when you receive payments from the plan: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/t4040/rrsps-other-registered-plans-retirement.html
- CRA, "First Home Savings Account (FHSA)" (date modified 2026-02-02) — registered plan for first-time home buyers to save tax-free; contributions generally deductible; $8,000 room in the first year: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- CRA, "Participating in your FHSAs" (date modified 2026-09-17) — $8,000 in the year you open your first FHSA; later years formula uses $8,000 and $40,000; carryforward up to $8,000: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account/contributing-your-fhsa.html
- CRA, "Withdrawals from your FHSAs" (date modified 2026-02-10) — qualifying withdrawal is tax-free and not included in income; taxable withdrawals are included in income: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account/withdrawing-money-from-your-fhsa.html
