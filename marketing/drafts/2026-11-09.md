## Sources checked (2026-11-09)

- CRA, "General Information for GST/HST Registrants" (RC4022, date modified 2026-01-07) — small supplier definition: revenue (with associates') from worldwide taxable supplies of $30,000 or less ($50,000 for public service bodies) in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters; going over in one quarter ends small supplier status from the supply that put you over (register within 29 days, tax charged on that supply); going over in four quarters ends it at the end of the following month; a small supplier that doesn't register generally doesn't charge GST/HST (except certain real property sales); taxi and commercial ride-sharing must register: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/rc4022/general-information-gst-hst-registrants.html
- CRA, "Register voluntarily for a GST/HST account" (date modified 2026-01-14) — you may register if you make taxable supplies in Canada; if registered you must charge, collect and remit GST/HST and file returns, may claim ITCs, and must stay registered at least one year; if you don't register you do not charge GST/HST (other than on certain taxable supplies of real property) and can't claim ITCs: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/account-register-voluntarily.html
