## Before posting

- **Who must charge QST is not covered on purpose.** Whether a business outside Quebec (or a small Quebec business) has to register for QST is Revenu Québec's rule, not CRA's, and couldn't be checked on canada.ca. The captions only say "check with Revenu Québec" — don't add "you must charge QST" anywhere.
- The $1,000 / $50.00 / $99.75 / $1,149.75 and the "wrong" $104.74 (9.975% × $1,050) are our own illustrative arithmetic on CRA's rates — keep the "Example" label on screen.
- The GST line only applies if you're GST/HST-registered (small suppliers don't charge GST — see day 9). Kept out of the video for length.
- CRA's rate page lists Quebec's 9.975% in its "PST" column and doesn't use the word "QST"; the "QST" name, "separate from the GST" and "without the GST" come from CRA's financial-institutions page. Fine to use, but a quick look at Revenu Québec's QST rate page before posting is cheap insurance.
- "GST + QST" tax preset is listed for the Invoice Generator in `site/src/products.json` — confirm the Payhip listing says the same.
