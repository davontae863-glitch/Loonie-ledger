# Marketing system

Organic only: Pinterest, TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and search. No paid ads, no fake engagement, no claims we can't back up.

## How a day works

1. A scheduled task (Claude) writes the day's draft to `drafts/YYYY-MM-DD.md` each morning: one Pinterest pin and one 30-second video script, both from `calendar.md`, plus captions and the tracked link.
2. Davontae reviews the draft, makes the pin (Canva free plan, using the text in the draft) or films the screen recording, and posts it.
3. Every post uses its **tracked link**, so the dashboard shows which posts bring visitors.
4. Each Monday's draft starts with a short review of last week's clicks (from the dashboard) and what to change.

## Tracked links

Format: `https://shop.loonieledger.workers.dev/go/<campaign>?to=<page>`

- `campaign`: lowercase letters, numbers and dashes, e.g. `pin-gst-threshold-01`
- `to`: a page on our site, e.g. `/gst-threshold-checker/`

The link counts the click, then sends the visitor to the page. It only ever sends people to our own site.

## Rules for every piece of content

- Facts about CRA rules, rates or limits must be checked against canada.ca on the day, with the source noted in the draft.
- No "passive income", "guaranteed", or "save thousands" claims.
- Products marked "Coming soon" are never promoted as available; point to the free GST checker or the product page's "Coming soon" status.
- No customer stories, reviews or numbers until they're real.
- Instagram and the Facebook Page post automatically every evening through Windsor.ai (official Meta APIs), approved by the owner on 2026-11-05 ("do it every day without my go-ahead"). Two scheduled tasks: "Loonie Ledger daily midday post" (12:23 PM ET, carousel on Instagram + image on Facebook, on the pin topic) and "Loonie Ledger daily auto-post" (7:37 PM ET, Reel on Instagram + Reel image on Facebook). Both log to `posted.md` and never post the same slot twice.
- YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Pinterest are posted by the owner (Buffer free plan). Windsor.ai reads their stats only.
- Auto-posts follow every rule above; if a fact can't be re-checked on canada.ca that day, the post is skipped and the owner is notified.

## Music on videos

Every video gets upbeat (120–128 BPM) background music mixed into the file before it's posted (Instagram's in-app music library can't be added through the posting API). The tracks are original instrumentals generated by `marketing/video/music/make_music.py` from plain synthesized tones, so we own them and there's nothing to license or get muted. There are 8 tracks and `marketing/video/add_music.sh in.mp4 out.mp4 [track]` rotates through them by date, so back-to-back days never share a soundtrack (pass a track number to pick one); `batch.cjs` and `make_video.cjs` do this automatically. Don't use songs downloaded from elsewhere unless their license clearly allows social media use.
