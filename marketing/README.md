# Marketing system

Organic only: Pinterest, TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and search. No paid ads, no fake engagement, no claims we can't back up.

## How a day works

1. A scheduled task (Claude) writes the day's draft to `drafts/YYYY-MM-DD.md` each morning: one Pinterest pin and one 30-second video script, both from `calendar.md`, plus captions and the tracked link.
2. Davontae reviews the draft, makes the pin (Canva free plan, using the text in the draft) or films the screen recording, and posts it.
3. Every post uses its **tracked link**, so the dashboard shows which posts bring visitors.
4. Each Monday's draft starts with a short review of last week's clicks (from the dashboard) and what to change.

## Tracked links

Format: `https://shop.loonieledger.workers.dev/go/<campaign>?to=<page>`

- `campaign`: lowercase letters, numbers and dashes, e.g. `pin-gst-threshold-01`
- `to`: a page on our site, e.g. `/gst-threshold-checker/`

The link counts the click, then sends the visitor to the page. It only ever sends people to our own site.

## Rules for every piece of content

- Facts about CRA rules, rates or limits must be checked against canada.ca on the day, with the source noted in the draft.
- No "passive income", "guaranteed", or "save thousands" claims.
- Products marked "Coming soon" are never promoted as available; point to the free GST checker or the product page's "Coming soon" status.
- No customer stories, reviews or numbers until they're real.
- Instagram and the Facebook Page post automatically every evening through Windsor.ai (official Meta APIs), approved by the owner on 2026-11-05 ("do it every day without my go-ahead"). Two scheduled tasks: "Loonie Ledger daily midday post" (12:23 PM ET, carousel on Instagram + image on Facebook, on the pin topic) and "Loonie Ledger daily auto-post" (7:37 PM ET, Reel on Instagram + Reel image on Facebook). Both log to `posted.md` and never post the same slot twice.
- YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Pinterest are posted by the owner (Buffer free plan). Windsor.ai reads their stats only.
- Auto-posts follow every rule above; if a fact can't be re-checked on canada.ca that day, the post is skipped and the owner is notified.

## Music on videos

Every video gets upbeat (120–128 BPM) background music mixed into the file before it's posted (Instagram's in-app music library can't be added through the posting API). The tracks are original instrumentals generated by `marketing/video/music/make_music.py` from plain synthesized tones, so we own them and there's nothing to license or get muted. There are 8 tracks and `marketing/video/add_music.sh in.mp4 out.mp4 [track]` rotates through them by date, so back-to-back days never share a soundtrack (pass a track number to pick one); `batch.cjs` and `make_video.cjs` do this automatically. Don't use songs downloaded from elsewhere unless their license clearly allows social media use.

## Voiceover (every other day)

Videos on even day-of-year dates (America/Edmonton) get a voiceover reading the on-screen text in our "Aspen" voice (owner's pick, 2026-11-05: a blend of Kokoro's af_heart and af_sarah — Kokoro is an open-source AI voice, Apache-2.0). Odd days are music-only. Run `marketing/video/get_tts.sh` once per session (downloads ~350 MB into ~/.cache), write the lines and the second each appears on screen to `marketing/video/daily/<date>-voice.json` (like `voice-lines-tfsa-withdrawal-trap.json`), then `python3 marketing/video/voiceover.py <with-outro.mp4> <lines.json> <out.mp4>` — it uses Aspen, rotates the music track by date, ducks the music under the voice and adds "Follow Loonie Ledger for more money tips." over the outro. Captions on voiced videos end with "🎙️ AI voiceover", and TikTok posts should have TikTok's AI-generated content toggle on (Meta and TikTok require labels for realistic AI audio). Never use the Piper "lessac" voice (research-only license) or anyone's cloned voice.

## Outro (every video)

Every video ends with the Loonie Ledger outro: `marketing/video/outro/outro.mp4` (2.8 s: the coin spins in with a coin "ding", then "Loonie Ledger", "Canadian money, made simple." and "Free tools & spreadsheets · link in bio"). Order: render the video → `add_outro.sh in.mp4 out.mp4` → `add_music.sh` (music only) or `voiceover.py` (voice + music; it adds the spoken sign-off "Follow Loonie Ledger for more money tips." over the outro). End cards say "Get it at Loonie Ledger" / "Free at Loonie Ledger" instead of a bare "Link in bio". Rebuild the outro with `python3 outro/make_ding.py && node outro/make_outro.cjs`.

## Hooks: beat the 3-second skip (owner request, 2026-11-06)

Early Reels lost most viewers in the first seconds (avg watch 4.5–6.4 s on ~22 s videos). Every new video follows these rules; they override the batch-1 look where they conflict.

1. **Frame 1 says it all.** The full hook is on screen at 100% opacity on the very first frame — no fade-in, no blank frame, no brand bar or logo in the first 3 s (the brand appears after the hook and in the outro).
2. **Max ~7 words, huge type** (≥120 px), high contrast, centred in the middle third of the screen (not under the Reels UI at the bottom). Use a dark spruce (#102a22 / #1d5a45) background for the hook scene so it stands out from the light feed; bronze highlight on the key word or number.
3. **Lead with the surprise, a number, or "you".** Patterns that work: a mistake ("Don't put it back yet"), a specific number ("$30,000 — without making $30,000"), a direct challenge ("Sort these 5 expenses"), or the result first. Never open with the brand, a greeting, or a slow setup.
4. **Motion from 0.0 s.** Something moves in the first half-second: a quick punch-in zoom (100%→106% over 0.6 s), a number counting up, or a word popping in — while the hook text itself is already readable.
5. **Sound from 0.0 s.** Voiced days: the first voice line starts at t = 0.0 and is the hook word-for-word. Music days: the track starts on the beat at frame 1 (it does already).
6. **Change something every ~1.5–2 s** after the hook (new line, new card, number reveal). No static frame longer than 2.5 s.
7. **Keep it short:** 12–18 s before the outro.
8. **Cover = the hook frame**, so the grid and the first frame match.
