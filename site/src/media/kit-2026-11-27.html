Can I throw out paper receipts after taking a photo? CRA receipt photo rules for self-employed Canadians
Description
Taking photos of receipts for your side hustle or small business in Canada? CRA says you generally must keep your paper originals — images replace them only if made to the CGSB (Canadian General Standards Board) standard. Emailed receipts must be kept in an electronic readable format, even if you print them, and CRA says you should back up your files. Not tax advice — check CRA for your situation.
