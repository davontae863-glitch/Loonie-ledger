TFSA, RRSP or FHSA? The tax break lands at different times: a TFSA has no deduction but is generally tax-free coming out, an RRSP gives a deduction but is generally taxed when you withdraw, and an FHSA gives a deduction and a tax-free qualifying withdrawal for your first home. 2026 limits from CRA: TFSA $7,000, RRSP up to $33,810, FHSA $8,000 in the year you open your first one ($40,000 lifetime). Not tax advice — check CRA for your situation.
