Title
How to number invoices: a simple invoice numbering system for freelancers in Canada (2026-001 format)
Description
Still numbering your invoices 1, 2, 3? Put the year first, then count up — 2026-001, 2026-002 — and start again at 2027-001 in January, so every invoice number stays unique, sorts in date order and a missing number is easy to spot. Voided an invoice? Keep the number and mark it VOID instead of reusing it. CRA lists sales and purchase invoices among the GST/HST records to keep, usually for 6 years from the end of the year they relate to. Not tax advice — check CRA for your situation.
