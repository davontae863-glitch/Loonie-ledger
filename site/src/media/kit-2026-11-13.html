How long to keep business receipts and records in Canada (CRA 6-year rule)
Description
How long do you have to keep business receipts in Canada? CRA says six years from the end of the last tax year they relate to — so a self-employed person's 2025 records stay until at least Dec 31, 2031. Filed late? Keep them six years from the date you filed, and destroying records early needs CRA's written permission (Form T137). Not tax advice — check CRA for your situation.
