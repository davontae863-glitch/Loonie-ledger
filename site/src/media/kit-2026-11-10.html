Can you claim your home office in Canada? Business-use-of-home expenses for self-employed
Description
Self-employed in Canada and working from home? You can deduct business-use-of-home expenses if the workspace is your principal place of business, or used only for business and regularly to meet clients. Claim the business part on a reasonable basis, like workspace area ÷ total home area — but these expenses can't create or increase a business loss; unused amounts carry forward. Claimed on Form T2125, line 9945. Not tax advice — check CRA for your situation.
