Title
Selling online in Canada? Digital platforms report your sales to CRA
Description
Sell goods, rent out a place, drive rideshare or do gig work through an app or website in Canada? Under CRA's Reporting Rules for Digital Platform Operators, platforms report certain sellers' name, address, tax number and sales by calendar quarter to CRA every year, and you get a copy by January 31. Sellers with fewer than 30 sales of goods and $2,800 or less aren't reported, but business income still goes on your tax return. Not tax advice — check CRA for your situation.
