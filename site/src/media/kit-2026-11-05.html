Your December bookkeeping checklist for Canadian sole proprietors
Description
Self-employed in Canada? CRA says self-employed individuals generally have to use a December 31 year-end, so December is your last month to tidy this year's books. Five things to do before January 1: (1) add up your sales for each calendar quarter and check them against the $30,000 GST/HST small-supplier threshold — one quarter, or the last four quarters added together; (2) write down each vehicle's odometer reading at year-end, since CRA asks for one at the start and end of the fiscal period; (3) match your bank statements and deposit slips to your income log; (4) for any expense with no receipt, note the seller's name and address, the amount, the date and what you bought; (5) list who still owes you and follow up on unpaid invoices.
