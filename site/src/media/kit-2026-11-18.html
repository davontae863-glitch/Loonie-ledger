How much TFSA, RRSP or FHSA contribution room do you really have? CRA updates the TFSA room in your CRA account once a year, in spring, so it doesn't include this year's deposits; your RRSP deduction limit and FHSA participation room are on your latest notice of assessment. Keep your own records of this year's contributions to avoid the 1%-a-month tax on excess amounts. Not tax advice — check CRA for your situation.
