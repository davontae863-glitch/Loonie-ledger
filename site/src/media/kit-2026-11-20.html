T2125 expense lines: where does a new desk go? (Canada self-employed)
Description
Self-employed in Canada and not sure which T2125 line an expense goes on? An online ad goes on line 8521, pens and stamps on 8810, the business share of cell airtime on 9220 and a client meal on 8523 (generally 50%). A desk or chair is a capital item, claimed over several years with capital cost allowance (line 9936). Not tax advice — check CRA for your situation.
