What is a small supplier for GST/HST in Canada? When you don't have to charge GST
Description
Not registered for GST/HST? Then you generally don't charge it. CRA calls you a small supplier when your taxable sales (plus your associates') are $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four calendar quarters. Register voluntarily and you must charge, collect and remit GST/HST, but you can claim input tax credits. Not tax advice — check CRA for your situation.
